## 🕹️ About

Order of the Sinking Star is a massive single-player puzzle adventure set across four strange worlds filled with magic, dangerous machines, monsters, and hidden connections. Play as a rotating cast of heroes, solve more than a thousand handcrafted puzzles, and uncover the mystery behind the Order. This Windows build is an unofficial, raw pre-release leak that surfaced ahead of the finished release.

For patient puzzle fans who enjoy deep systems, methodical exploration, strange worlds, and raw early builds.
